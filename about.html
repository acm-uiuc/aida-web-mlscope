About

Our Mission:

We seek to educate UIUC students on the fundamentals and state of the art topics in machine learning and data science. We have weekly/biweekly problems that ramp up in difficulty and give a large breadth.

For Students:

We accept everyone as a member to the organization, as long as you are willing to learn and treat other members kindly with respect. There is no formal process to join the club, simply join the Discord server and we will post when our events are in the announcements channel. As of Spring 2025, we meet on Sundays at 1310 DCL, 12:30pm.

For Companies:

We are always looking for companies to sponsor our club and provide us with resources to help our members learn. If you are interested in sponsoring us, please reach out by emailing handzel4@illinois.edu.
